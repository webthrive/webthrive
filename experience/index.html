Journey

// Overview //

I've dedicated my career to helping B2B, SaaS, and AI companies excel in digital marketing and demand generation. With over two decades of experience, I specialize in creating data-driven strategies that deliver predictable growth in today's rapidly evolving AI and LLM search landscape.

Early Foundations

// Late 1990s –
Early 2000s

My journey began during the dot-com era, where I cut my teeth on early digital marketing campaigns and watched the internet transform from a novelty into a business imperative. This early exposure gave me a front-row seat to the evolution of digital channels and consumer behavior.

Through various roles in agencies and startups, I developed a deep understanding of both creative storytelling and data-driven performance marketing—a combination that would become the foundation of my approach to CMO leadership.

// 2006–2008 //

After college, I partnered with several real estate startups, blending web development with early digital marketing to drive leads. That's where I learned how powerful online visibility could be for real businesses.

The E-Commerce Era

// 2008–2015 //

Head of Marketing, FaucetDepot.com

This was my deep dive into performance marketing at scale. As Head of Marketing, I helped grow FaucetDepot.com into a $25M-a-year ecommerce business, managing large ad budgets and more than 80,000 SKUs across multiple digital channels.

This period is where I built my foundation in analytics, optimization, and operational scale — understanding how data, product, and revenue all connect.

SaaS & AI Transition

// 2016 – Present //

Around 2017, I shifted my focus to SaaS and AI, drawn by the pace of innovation and the challenge of scaling new technology. I began consulting for early-stage startups, helping them build their GTM engines and digital foundations from scratch.

Director of Demand Generation, Functionize

At Functionize, I've led digital demand generation for an AI-powered software testing platform through multiple funding rounds, including a $41M Series B. From paid and organic programs to messaging and inbound systems, I've helped the company grow from seed to scale.

// Current //

Head of Marketing, TheReportingHub.com

Today, I also lead marketing and growth for The Reporting Hub — a SaaS platform focused on data distribution and AI agent customization. My focus here is on positioning, demand generation, and brand development as the company scales rapidly.

// Today //

These days, I split my time leading digital strategy at Functionize and The Reporting Hub while advising early-stage SaaS startups.

I'm known for blending technical fluency, creative brand thinking, and data discipline to build marketing systems that don't just scale — they last.
